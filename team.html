Adam B. Rai

Founder & Lead Coach

Adam brings a deep expertise in helping young adults navigate ADHD and executive functioning challenges. Having trained under a Doctor of Neurobiology, Adam brings a blend of science, structure, and empathy to every session.

His coaching approach is rooted in consistency, clarity, and real-world tools that help clients build focus, motivation, and self-direction. Adam is especially passionate about supporting students in transitional moments, whether it's adjusting to college life, managing independence, or developing lasting habits.
