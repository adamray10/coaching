Get Started
"BODH (bode) is a hindi word meaning awareness and enlightenment which we seek to instill in our clients."

ADHD coaching is a transformative process that helps young adults build the focus, confidence, and skills they need to thrive. As a parent, you want your son or daughter to flourish both academically and personally. Specialized coaching provides the guidance to navigate this pivotal stage of life with greater ease.

Through personalized sessions, an ADHD coach supports young adults in clarifying their goals, managing challenges like procrastination, overwhelm, or inconsistent motivation, and developing practical strategies for success. This process not only enhances self-awareness and resilience but also cultivates a growth mindset that empowers them to excel in school and beyond.

ADHD coaching is more than problem-solving; it equips your young adult with tools to lead a life filled with purpose and confidence. By offering structure, accountability, and steady encouragement, coaching helps them build habits and skills to manage life's complexities. Together, we create a path toward lasting independence and meaningful progress.

Meet Adam B. Rai, Founder

Adam is a dedicated coach who specializes in empowering young adults, particularly those navigating the challenges of ADHD and Executive Functioning. Drawing from his own experiences and mentorship under a Doctor of Neurobiology, Adam combines personal insight with a deep understanding of the psychology of motivation. His coaching emphasizes consistency, positivity, and structure, equipping his clients with the tools they need to build confidence, stay focused, and achieve their goals.

With a professional background that spans acting, stand-up comedy, and appearances on HBO, Amazon, and Comedy Central stages, Adam brings a unique perspective to his coaching. His journey through the entertainment industry has taught him the value of persistence, self-belief, and resilience—lessons he passionately shares with his clients.

Adam's coaching is about more than goal-setting; it's about transformation. He asks the pivotal question, "What do you want to create for yourself?" and works as an accountability partner to help clients discover their potential, tackle obstacles, and build a life they're proud of. Whether supporting parents of young adults seeking balance and focus or guiding individuals toward their next chapter, Adam is committed to helping his clients thrive.
